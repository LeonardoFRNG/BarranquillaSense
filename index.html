Before we start

The city, through my senses.

A city is not only something you look at. Barranquilla reaches you through the river breeze, the beat of a drum, the smell of food from a kiosk, the warmth of the sun and the first bite of something typical. This guide follows those sensations across six places.

01 Riverside promenade

Stop 01 of 06

Gran Malecón del Río

Nature, culture and the river.

Sight

I see a beautiful view of the Magdalena River, colorful sculptures and a modern boardwalk.

Sound

I hear the waves, music, people laughing and the rhythm of the city.

Smell

I smell the fresh river breeze and delicious food from the nearby kiosks.

Touch

I feel the warm sun, the fresh air and the cool breeze from the river.

Taste

I taste fresh juices, coconut and typical snacks like arepa de huevo.

Climate

It's hot and sunny, with a pleasant breeze from the river.

Emotion

I feel happy, relaxed and proud of my city.

02 Urban landmark

Stop 02 of 06

La Ventana al Mundo

A symbol of our identity.

Sight

I see a huge, colorful sculpture that represents the diversity and joy of Barranquilla.

Sound

I hear the cars, the music of nearby vendors and people taking photos.

Smell

I smell the sea breeze and the fresh air.

Touch

I feel the warm sun on my skin and the energy of the place.

Taste

I taste local snacks like patacones and fresh coconut.

Climate

It's hot, sunny and breezy.

Emotion

I feel excited, proud and full of energy.

03 Historic neighborhood

Stop 03 of 06

Barrio El Prado

History, architecture and elegance.

Sight

I see beautiful colonial houses, tree-lined streets and elegant architecture.

Sound

I hear the sound of birds, quiet footsteps and soft city life.

Smell

I smell the flowers, fresh air and the aroma of nearby cafés.

Touch

I feel the cool shade of the trees and the calm of the neighborhood.

Taste

I taste coffee and traditional desserts like cocadas.

Climate

It's warm and pleasant, with a gentle breeze.

Emotion

I feel peace, nostalgia and admiration for its history.

04 Public square

Stop 04 of 06

Plaza de la Paz

Where the culture comes alive.

Sight

I see colorful sculptures, green areas and people enjoying the square.

Sound

I hear vallenato, cumbia, drums and the joy of the people.

Smell

I smell the typical food, flowers and fresh air.

Touch

I feel the vibrant energy and the warm sun.

Taste

I taste empanadas, arepas and local sweets.

Climate

It's hot and sunny, typical of the Caribbean.

Emotion

I feel joy, pride and cultural connection.

05 Popular market

Stop 05 of 06

Mercado de Barranquillita

Colors, flavors and tradition.

Sight

I see vibrant colors, fresh fruits, crafts and local products.

Sound

I hear the voices of the vendors, music and the hustle and bustle.

Smell

I smell fresh food, spices, fish and tropical fruits.

Touch

I feel the energy of the people and the texture of local products.

Taste

I taste fresh fruits and typical dishes like mote de queso and seafood.

Climate

It's hot and humid, very Caribbean.

Emotion

I feel curiosity, happiness and a desire to explore more.

06 Wildlife park

Stop 06 of 06

Zoológico de Barranquilla

Nature and wildlife.

Sight

I see amazing animals like giraffes, lions, tigers and colorful birds.

Sound

I hear the animals, birds and children laughing.

Smell

I smell the fresh air and nature.

Touch

I feel the shade of the trees and the texture of the environment.

Taste

I taste snacks like ice cream and fresh juices from the stands.

Climate

It's hot and humid, with lots of sunshine.

Emotion

I feel amazed, happy and connected with nature.
